PuzzleVybe · Social queue

__HEADING__

Three puzzles. Each has a 3-slide Instagram carousel (the art, hands putting it together, the finished puzzle on a table), a 9:16 video for Reels and TikTok, and a caption for each platform.

Approve

Mark a post approved, copy its captions, and load the image or video into your scheduler. Each batch also arrives as a zip in the chat.

Needs changes

Tell Claude in the chat what to change (the hook, the tone, a hashtag) and it will redo that post.

Next batches

New batches arrive on a schedule with a fresh set of books, skipping anything featured recently.
